Hormonal Health 101

Know what you're tracking

🌺

PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome affects 1 in 10 women. Early signs include irregular cycles, acne and weight changes.

🦋

Hypothyroidism

An underactive thyroid causes fatigue, weight gain and slower metabolism — easy to miss for years.

🌙

PMDD

Severe pre-menstrual mood disruption affecting 3–8% of women — distinct from typical PMS.

🍯

Insulin Resistance

Often a precursor to PCOS and Type 2 diabetes. Cravings and post-meal fatigue are early hints.
